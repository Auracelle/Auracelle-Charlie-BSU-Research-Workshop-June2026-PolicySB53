E-AGPO-HT v3.1

Seven governance domains. One score.

The g-GWC (General Governance Wargaming Capacity) aggregates seven Broad Governance Capability domains scored in real time as actor moves are logged. A Kalman filter tracks trajectory and projects forward three sessions.

SADSecurity Architecture Design

Research Design

Five research KPIs per session.

KPI 1
Multilateralism
Coalition rate across six rounds. Does the whistleblower clause hold a five-actor coalition?
KPI 2
Stress Test Effectiveness
g-GWC shift pre/post Red Team shock. Measures policy resilience under adversarial pressure.
KPI 3
Process Acceleration
Accel(AGPO) vs. 18-month traditional policy review baseline. Speed advantage of simulation.
KPI 4
Foresight Sustainability
Kalman trend direction. Is the governance framework improving or declining across rounds?
KPI 5
Weight Sensitivity
Pareto-optimal BGC configuration from MARL engine. Which domain levers matter most?
Research Context

Doctoral research at Bath Spa University.

Principal Investigator: Grace-Alice Evans — Founder, Auracelle AI Governance Labs. Doctoral candidate, Bath Spa University (Supervisor: Dr. John Curry). Thesis: Testing Public Policy Outcomes through War Gaming Methodologies for Strengthening AI Governance.

Framework: E-AGPO-HT v3.1 (Evaluative Agentic Governance Policy Optimisation — Hybrid Taxonomy). Three-stratum latent variable model with SEM fit indices (CFI ≥ 0.95, RMSEA ≤ 0.06). Kalman filter tracks g-GWC across rounds with forward projection and uncertainty bands.

Research Objective 3: Conduct experimental policy tests using differentiated wargame compositions to assess how participant identity, professional experience, and affiliation shape policy negotiation outcomes. Composition group (Sector × Gender) is the primary independent variable across sessions.
