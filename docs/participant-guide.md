# Auracelle Charlie — Pilot Workshop Participant Guide

**Wargaming and AI Governance · SB-53 Stress-Test · June 7–14, 2026**

---

## What you are doing — in plain language

You are going to stress-test California Senate Bill 53 — the first enforceable AI safety
law in the United States. This session focuses on one specific provision: the whistleblower
protection clause. SB-53 requires covered AI developers to establish anti-retaliation
protections and anonymous reporting channels for employees who identify safety concerns.

Five AI agents negotiate this provision under adversarial conditions. Your job is to guide
and record their moves. No prior knowledge of AI law is required.

---

## What is at stake — the whistleblower question

Every actor in this simulation has a different stake in whether the whistleblower provision
survives:

- **State / Government Actors** — Must decide whether federal preemption nullifies
  California whistleblower protections.
- **Civil Society Actors** — Treat the whistleblower clause as a non-negotiable red line.
- **Industry Actors** — Will seek to narrow "protected disclosure" to exclude competitive
  intelligence and commercially sensitive safety data.
- **EU / International Teams** — Push for alignment with EU Whistleblower Directive
  (2019/1937) standards.
- **Academic / Technical Actors** — Question whether the anonymous reporting channel can
  be technically secured. Propose independent third-party channel operators.

## Step 4 — Play six rounds in Tab ② (40–50 minutes)

You are now in Facilitator Mode. Each round follows this sequence:

1. **Policy Owner Agent fires.** Read the challenge about the whistleblower provision.
2. **Actor Guidance panel updates** (🤖 above Log Move). Read the actor's position
   and suggested rationale. Use it or write your own.
3. **Select the Move Type.** The guidance panel suggests one per round — you can override.
4. **Click ▶ Log Move.** The form advances automatically to the next actor.
5. **Repeat for all five actors.** When all five are logged the Advance Round button
   pulses. Click it.

**⚡ Rounds 4 and 5 — Red Team shock events:**
The Red Team Agent fires at Round 4 (AI Incident) and Round 5 (Unilateral Withdrawal).
A red alert banner appears. Continue logging moves as normal.

## Move type reference

| Move Type | When to use | Effect |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| PROPOSE | Tabling a new position | Raises NDM and ESI |
| COALITION | Joining another actor's position | Raises NDM coalition score |
| COUNTER | Rejecting and offering alternative | Mixed: raises ESI, lowers NDM |
| COMPLY | Accepting the framework | Raises IIC and NDM |
| ESCALATE | Invoking a red line | Raises SRA, lowers coalition |
| DEFECT | Withdrawing from coalition | Lowers NDM and ASI significantly |
