Auracelle Charlie v6.0 · E-AGPO-HT Framework

A war game for AI governance — without needing to be an expert.

Auracelle Charlie puts you in the room where AI policy gets made. You play a real stakeholder — a government, a tech company, a civil society group — and negotiate a live governance scenario with other actors. No prior knowledge required. The simulation guides you through every step and scores the outcome automatically.

By the end of a session, you will have stress-tested a real AI policy, discovered where it breaks under pressure, and contributed to doctoral research on how different groups approach governance challenges.

What you get out of this exercise

🔬
See how AI policy actually breaks
Real governance frameworks — like California's SB-53 AI safety law — look solid on paper. The simulation reveals where they collapse under adversarial pressure, defection, or crisis. You will see the exact moment a coalition fractures and why.
🤝
Experience multilateral negotiation firsthand
You play one of five real actors: a government, a tech company, a civil society organisation, a military bloc, or an international institution. Each has different interests. The simulation measures whether the group reaches a stable agreement — or if someone defects.
📊
Generate evidence, not just opinions
Charlie scores every round using the E-AGPO-HT mathematical engine — measuring coalition strength, defection signals, governance resilience, and policy acceleration. Your session contributes real data to doctoral research at Bath Spa University and UC Berkeley.

About your data and your group selection

The participant group you selected at login — — — is the primary independent variable in this research. The study is examining whether professional background shapes how people approach AI governance negotiations. Your session data (moves, outcomes, scores) is stored anonymously and used only for this doctoral research. No names or contact details are ever associated with session records. You may withdraw at any time by closing your browser.

What is inside Charlie — the eleven tabs explained

Charlie v6.0 — Tab Reference (plain language)
①
Session Design
Where you start. Choose your governance scenario (AI weapons, cyber norms, nuclear-AI nexus, frontier AI, and others), your composition group, and how many rounds to run. This is your mission briefing.
②
Facilitator Mode
The live game table. Each actor takes a turn — propose, negotiate, form a coalition, defect, comply, or escalate. The Policy Owner AI agent fires automatically here, issuing governance challenges and assessing your moves in real time.
③
Governance Indicators
The scoreboard. Seven domains of governance capability are tracked live: Strategic Threat Intelligence, Security Architecture, Simulation Intelligence, Negotiation Dynamics, Strategic Rationality, Institutional Capacity, and Adaptive Scalability.
④
Foresight Validation
Compares your session outcome against historical cases — the EU AI Act, GDPR, Bletchley Summit, California SB-53. Shows where your negotiation tracked or diverged from precedent, and projects the likely trajectory forward using a Kalman filter.
⑤
After-Action Review
Your session report. Generates the PSTOA (Policy Stress-Test Outcome Assessment) score and a full exportable JSON record. This is the file that uploads to the Charlie Lab for deeper statistical analysis.
⑥
SIPRI Data
Live military and defence data from the Stockholm International Peace Research Institute. Used to ground actor positions in real-world capability data when playing military or security-focused scenarios.
⑦
MARL Engine
Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning. Runs the AI-versus-AI learning engine that finds optimal governance strategies across thousands of simulated sessions. Shows the theoretical best and worst outcomes for the scenario.
⑧
Autonomous Sim
Fully automated simulation mode. No human input needed — five AI agents run the complete negotiation. Useful for generating baseline data and exploring scenario outcomes before a live session. This is what the Demo uses.
⑨
Red Team & Shocks
The stress-test engine. Inject nine types of crisis into a running session: data breach, unilateral withdrawal, AI incident, sanctions, cyberattack, media crisis, industry defection, great power veto, or classified leak. Measures how far the policy moves under each shock.
⑩
Agentic AI
The AI agent configuration panel. Paste your Anthropic API key here to activate the Policy Owner agent (always-on governance challenger) and the Red Team agent (adversarial pressure). Uses Claude Sonnet 4. Key is stored in browser memory only — never persisted.
⑪
Session Archive
Cross-session tracking. Stores all your previous sessions in browser local storage so you can compare how outcomes shift across different scenarios, compositions, and shock conditions over time.
